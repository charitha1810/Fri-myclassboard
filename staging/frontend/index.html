New here? Key words used on this page
  • MCB gateway / online via MCB: parents paid online through a payment gateway connected to MyClassBoard (the “Online Payment” mode).
  • Our partner gateway: a gateway MCB has a partnership with. MCB earns commission only on these payments.
  • Not our partner: parents pay online, but MCB earns nothing on those payments.
  • No MCB gateway: the school or branch collects every fee outside MCB.
  • Offline: cash, card swipe at the counter, cheque and DD. MCB earns nothing until these move to pay links.
  • ₹ amounts are shown in full, to the paisa, exactly as the database adds them up (chart scales are marked in ₹ Cr: 1 Cr = ₹1,00,00,000). % of fees always means a share of the total fees collected for the group shown.
  • Hover over any number, bar or column heading to see more detail.

Grow MCB commission

MCB earns commission only on fees paid online through our partner gateways. Below are four ways to get more fees onto them, with the extra commission each could bring in a year.
Commission we earn now (per year)
How these figures are worked out
  • Commission we earn now = commission rate × fees paid online through our partner gateways, scaled to a full year.
  • Each “way” assumes the target share (see the note above) of the money involved moves onto our partner gateways.
  • See top institutes lists the schools with the most to gain. List them all opens them in the Institutes page.
  • Change the rate with “MCB commission rate” in the filter bar, or the default in Revenue settings.
commission = Σ(PG volume × partner fraction) × take × 12/months · partner fraction = share of an org's mapped gateways with IsActive 1 AND IsMCBPartner 1 · levers: conv% × offline (cash, POS, cheque, DD) at partner orgs · non-partner PG volume · N/A-branch share × collections × conv% · N/A orgs' collections × conv%

How to read this
  • The coloured bar shows how many schools are in each group. The number and % are printed on the bar and repeated in the key underneath.
  • In each box, “60% of all 1,237” means 60% of all schools are in this group.

How to read this
  • We earn on this: paid through the MCB gateway or loan partners. MCB earns commission.
  • Offline: can move online: cash, card swipe, cheque and DD at the counter. This is the biggest opportunity.
  • Online, but not via MCB: NEFT, direct UPI or BBPS straight to the school's bank. MCB earns nothing.
  • Auto-debit: automatic bank debits (ECS / NACH).
  • % of fees is the method's share of all fees this year. The last two columns compare the latest month with the month before.
  • Colours of the change: violet = good for MCB (money we earn on is growing, or offline money is shrinking); orange = bad for MCB.
